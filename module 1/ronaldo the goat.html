Few facts about the greatest of all time

cristiano ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro (born February 5, 1985, Madeira, Portugal) is a legendary footballer, widely regarded as one of the greatest of all time. He currently plays for Al-Nassr in Saudi Arabia and captains the Portugal national team.

Career Highlights

  • Sporting CP (2002–2003): Professional debut.
  • Manchester United (2003–2009, 2021–2022) : 3 Premier League titles, 1 Champions League, Ballon d’Or (2008).
  • Real Madrid (2009–2018): Club’s all-time top scorer (450+ goals), 4 Champions Leagues, 2 La Liga titles.
  • Juventus (2018–2021): 2 Serie A titles.
  • Al-Nassr (2023–present): Over 100 goals and Saudi Pro League Champion (2026).

International Success

  • Portugal Caps/Goals: 226 appearances, 143 goals
  • Euro 2016: First-ever European Championship (Euros) win for Portugal.
  • Nations League: Winner in 2019 and 2025.
  • World Cup: Played in 5 editions (2006–2022).
